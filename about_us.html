From One Room to One Million

In 2020, a young mentor named “M Kiran Sagar” sat in a small room in Hyderabad with a single belief that a student from a rural village in Andhra Pradesh or Telangana deserved the same quality of tech education as anyone else in the country. Not in a foreign language. Not at an unaffordable price. But in their language, at their pace, for their future.

That belief became PythonLife.

What started as a simple mission to teach technology in Telugu has grown into a movement. Today, PythonLife offers 100+ free software courses on YouTube, along with live and recorded programs in Python, Data Science, AI, DevOps, SQL and Java, all while staying true to the values we were founded on: accessibility, affordability, and authenticity.

The Vision Behind PythonLife

Kiran is more than a CEO — he is a Mentor, a Senior Technical Trainer, a YouTuber, a Career counsellor, and the visionary force behind everything PythonLife stands for.

His vision is simple yet powerful: make high-quality coding education affordable and accessible to every Telugu-speaking student , taught in their own language, at their own pace, with the guidance to build a successful tech career.

Through PythonLife, he focuses on practical learning, career guidance, and real-world skills that help students confidently prepare for the technology industry.

His goal is to bridge the gap between learning technology and building a successful career , helping students turn their knowledge into meaningful opportunities .

His mission is not just to teach coding, but to transform careers .
